How We Work

White-glove advisory, your terms

However we work together, everything is based on your situation, your family, and your goals. Regardless of what you choose, you get careful attention to detail, honest recommendations, and someone who listens before advising.

For when you are not sure where to begin. Start with a conversation.

Hourly Consulting

A focused session on your most pressing digital security, privacy, or technology question. Advice, education, and a clear recommendation you can act on.

Clients use these sessions to make sense of a suspicious email, talk through a concern about a device, get expert eyes on a decision before committing, or simply have someone calm their nerves and walk through a situation that feels off.

When you know the outcome you want, we handle it from start to finish.

Scoped Engagement

A complete engagement built around a defined result with a clear beginning and end. We assess your situation, design the plan, and implement it for you. Common examples include a full digital security overhaul, a complete Digital Continuity Plan, or an intentional technology reset.

Delivered white-glove, with a clear record of what was done and how it works, so you stay in control.

For clients who want an ongoing advisor rather than a one-time fix.

Ongoing Protection

We monitor your digital exposure, keep protections current as the landscape changes, and stay available as a private channel for the questions that come up in daily life. Should you click this link? Is this email real? Is there a new risk you should know about? We help you make informed decisions without your having to become the expert.
