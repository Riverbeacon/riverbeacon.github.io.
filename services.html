Our Work

White-glove advisory on your terms

We have three areas of work. Below are a few examples, not a complete list.

Pillar One

Privacy & Digital Security

Encrypted Messaging

Do you know how to send sensitive information across the internet? Most people don't. Regular email and text messages travel in plain text that others along the way can read and keep forever. We set you up with simple, practical tools so you can send bank details, documents and family information without exposure.

Phone Number Takeover Protection

Hackers can transfer your phone number to a device they control, then use it to defeat the security on your bank and email accounts. It's called a SIM swap, and it happens every day. We lock this door before anyone walks through it.

Password Strategy

Your entire digital life is built on a handful of passwords, and most people have them written down or reused everywhere. Done wrong, you either expose everything or lock yourself out of your own accounts. We build you a rock solid master password, a trusted password manager and a backup plan with safety built in, so you're covered from every direction.

Pillar Two

Digital Life Continuity

Digital Continuity Plan

When the unexpected happens, your family shouldn't have to navigate a digital maze. Most people's digital lives are scattered across dozens of accounts nobody else can find. We build a complete map of where everything lives, including the things money can't replace, like family photos and journals. Everything is backed up in more than one place, so no fire or locked account can erase it.

Password Vault Handoff Setup

Could your family get into your accounts if you couldn't help them? You don't want to hand over all your passwords now. We set up your password manager so people you trust can request access in an emergency. This includes a safe waiting period you control, so a request while you're living changes nothing. If you can't respond, access transfers on your terms. Nobody can take your passwords now, and nobody faces a locked vault later.

Bitcoin Inheritance Setup

Holding your own Bitcoin means total personal control, but that same irrevocable ownership becomes a problem without an effective setup for inheritance or emergencies. Your wallet key works as usual while you're living and a loved one's key can activate only after a period of inactivity you define, which is re-confirmed by your regular check ins. No one can touch it early, and no lawyer or custodian is needed to hand it off.

Pillar Three

Intentional Technology

The Digital Declutter

Are there apps on your phone you never open, notifications that interrupt you a hundred times a day, accounts you forgot you had? Each one is a small tax on your attention and your security. We help you take a hard look at all of it, and you decide what stays and what goes. Then we handle the cleanup and tighten the settings on what you keep. This isn't about becoming a monk. It's about clearing the runway.

Personal AI Strategy and Implementation

There's a lot of unfounded hype around AI. We cut all that out and get down to what it can actually do for you: taking over the rote tasks, speeding up recurring processes and freeing you to think with greater depth. We look at the real problems first, then decide whether AI is the right solution. If it is, we set it up and teach you to use it well, so the tools sharpen your judgment instead of replacing it.

Household Screen Time Setup

If you're concerned about what screens are doing to your household, you're not alone. Once you set the direction and rules you want in place, we translate those decisions into real configurations on the phones and tablets. These are meaningful and helpful limits that hold up over time and don't erode into extra taps and workarounds. The goal isn't restriction. It's a household that is more calm, capable and happy.

Something on your mind that is not listed here? Tell us what is worrying you and we will address it.
