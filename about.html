Why We Exist

The Realization

I spent years working in data analytics. One day, as I was reviewing patient datasets for a healthcare client, I realized how many real and intimate details of people's lives were stored in our databases. Prescriptions, divorces, and medical conditions were visible to anyone with access. This was all legal, but very exposing. This shifted my perspective. I began to see how organizations collected all kinds of data, but individuals frequently had no opt-out or control. Even those who collected data with good intentions often got hacked, and the victims could do nothing to prevent or fix it.

Loss of Control

This led to deeper questions about my own data. Who was collecting it? What were they using it for? When a cloud service provider locked me out of my account unexpectedly, I felt violated. I'd given them my data, and they controlled every part of it, including my access. I never wanted to feel that loss of control again.

Learning the Hard Way

So I tried DIY workarounds instead, but I made many rookie mistakes. I almost locked myself out of my own encrypted vault. This was one of the most stressful moments of my life. Fortunately, I found technical guides, mentors, and communities that helped me find practical ways to get control of my data and apps.

The Awakening

As I gained more control and became more intentional with my data and the apps I was using, I became more aware of a problem affecting many around me. Their screens constantly commanded their attention. Tools meant to help them be focused and productive were having the opposite effect. I saw coworkers and friends become unproductive and unhappy with the very technology that should have helped them. This inspired me to draw on my own reading, writing, and experience and start teaching groups about a more intentional approach. This began my journey of building solutions to ensure technology serves the user, not the other way around.

Our Philosophy

We approach your journey toward controlling your data and technology with a practical and proactive attitude. We've done the research and testing so you don't have to. Methods that don't work get discarded and those that do work get refined. We focus on your goals and bring them to life without burdening you with the technical work.

Decisions on this topic are personal. Each person balances convenience, control, focus and fun differently. Whatever setup you want as a user, we help you achieve it. Our role is to give you the tools to make informed choices and bring your vision of an ideal digital life to fruition.

That's why we're here.
